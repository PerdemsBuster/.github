# Maradona Legends ⚽💫

Welcome to the official GitHub of **Maradona Legends**, a Web3 initiative that immortalizes the legacy of Diego Armando Maradona through the power of digital collectibles, blockchain technology, and interactive fan engagement.

## 🌟 Mission

Our mission is to bridge the past and the future of football culture by transforming iconic moments, memories, and memorabilia of Diego Maradona into unique, verified digital assets — all while building a global community of collectors, fans, and storytellers.

## 🧩 What We're Building

Maradona Legends is a multi-platform project, offering a rich, interactive experience across various fronts:

- **[The Tap Experience](https://tap.maradonalegends.com/)**: A gamified minting experience where users interact with the legacy of Maradona to unlock exclusive NFTs.
- **[The Dashboard](https://app.maradonalegends.com/clients/dashboard)**: Your personal hub to track NFT assets, rewards, and community engagement.
- **[Official Website](https://www.maradonalegends.com/)**: Explore the collection, dive into Maradona’s story, and stay updated on drops, events, and community news.

All digital assets are deployed on **Starknet**, enabling scalability, transparency, and lower fees with a focus on sustainability and accessibility.

## 🔧 Tech Stack

- Blockchain: Starknet (zk-Rollup on Ethereum)
- Smart Contracts: Cairo
- Frontend: React, TypeScript
- Backend: Node.js, PostgreSQL
- Infrastructure: IPFS, Pinata
- Auth: Web3 Wallets (WalletConnect, Argent, etc.)

## 🚀 Project Status

We are currently in **Phase I of rollout**, which includes:
- Live minting through *The Tap*
- User dashboard with asset management tools
- Community activation campaigns and whitelist access

Upcoming milestones:
- Marketplace integrations
- AR/VR collectible experiences
- DAO governance structure
